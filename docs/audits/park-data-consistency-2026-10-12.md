## Source paths and current AI routing

- `prisma/seed-data/parks-raw.ts` supplies legacy fee fields. `prisma/seed-data/parks.ts` maps raw seed and corrected coordinates; `prisma/seed.ts` persists hours, province and coordinates, but **does not persist fees**.
- `lib/services/park-service.ts` reads Park rows. AI V2 `getParkInfo` reads hours from that service and exact fees/contact by canonical slug from `lib/data/park-fees.ts` and `lib/data/park-addresses.ts`.
- `data/knowledge/parks/*.md` is the file source for RAG; `KnowledgeDocument`/`KnowledgeChunk` is the live indexed copy. `getParkKnowledge` also attaches structured facts for fee, hours and contact questions.
- `lib/chat/core/park-catalog.ts` reads `data/knowledge/knowledge-index.json` for park identity and province metadata.
